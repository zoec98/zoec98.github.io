Hello, I am Zoé. I write smut.

You can find me on [DeviantArt](https://www.deviantart.com/zoec98) and on [GitHub](https://github.com/zoec98). This site gives my stories an additional home outside of DeviantArt.

On GitHub you can

- find the [source for this site](https://github.com/zoec98/zoec98.github.io), 
- [storyscraper](https://github.com/zoec98/story-scraper), a tool for downloading stories from several smut sites and turning them into EPUBs,
- and [Imagegen](https://github.com/zoec98/imagegen), a tool for generating AI images with `fal.ai`, with both a command-line utility for automation and a Flask application for a more comfortable workflow.
