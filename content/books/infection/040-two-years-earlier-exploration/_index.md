# Two years earlier: Exploration

Back at home,
Mara quickly realized how much she needed to adjust to life with the iGuardian.
The device's constant grip on her body left little room for error,
and she soon understood that even the simplest movements could cause discomfort if not done correctly.

During the week she was fitted with the device, Philippe was hosting a formal dinner.
Mara's job was to ensure the household staff prepared everything perfectly.
She moved carefully through the grand hallway, her heels clicking against the marble floor.
Anytime she shifted her weight the wrong way, the iGuardian’s inserts pressed against her insides uncomfortably,
reminding her to maintain the correct posture—hips straight, chest out, shoulders back.

If she slouched or moved to much against the device, she received a correction.
The device seemed to be learning,
adjusting its responses to achieve more with less.

As she entered the dining room, she noticed the staff setting the table.
When she approached, the soft rustle of her skirt was the only sound.
The staff looked up and curtsied respectfully.

Mara began to inspect the table setting.
As she leaned forward slightly to check the alignment of the silverware, she didn't push her back out far enough,
and the inserts made themselves felt as she moved her hip out of alignment.
She quickly adjusted her posture, pushing her hips back into the exaggerated pose the iGuardian demanded of her.
The shoulder braces forced her to push her chest out even further, reminding her not to bend the wrong way.
Every movement became a careful balancing act she had to master,
as if she were learning to stand, walk, and move all over again, always maintaining her pose.

She signed her approval to Claire, the head of staff, her hands moving gracefully despite the strain.
Claire nodded and returned to her work.
Mara hesitated, feeling the relentless pressure of the corset around her torso and the strain in her back.
The fact that she couldn’t bend or slouch without triggering a correction was frustrating.
To cope, she straightened herself even more, focusing on her breathing to calm her nerves.
She relied on her ballet and yoga training to maintain her posture.

The real challenge came when she had to sit.
As she approached the Victorian chair at the head of the table, she carefully lowered herself onto the seat.
Almost immediately, she felt discomfort as her body’s weight pressed against the crotch piece of the iGuardian.
The pressure pushed the inserts deeper into her, filling her to the point of pain,
and the iGuardian's corset body was forced upward,
compressing her ribs even more and pushing the breast covers uncomfortably into her chest.
The neck corset, in turn, was pushed up, poking into her chin and the back of her neck.

Mara quickly realized she couldn’t sit like this.
Shifting forward in the chair, she balanced herself on the edge of the seat,
placing her weight on her thighs instead of her crotch.
The discomfort eased slightly, allowing her to breathe more freely.
She folded her gloved hands in her lap, her knees pressed together by the underskirt.
This position, though challenging,
forced her into a very feminine and composed pose that matched the Victorian dress she wore perfectly.

When Philippe entered the room, his gaze immediately settled on Mara.
He paused, taking in her carefully maintained posture as she sat.

"Mara, is everything ready for the guests?" he asked.

Without turning or shifting her pose, Mara signed, "Yes, Sir. Everything is prepared as you instructed."

Philippe walked closer, his eyes scanning the table before returning to her rigid form.
He noticed how she balanced on the edge of the chair, her posture carefully held.

"Good," he said, his eyes appraising her. "I like what the iGuardian does for your posture. It suits you."

Mara signed back, "Thank you, Sir," though her hands trembled slightly as she struggled to maintain the position.
The strain of holding herself so rigidly, combined with the pressure from the device, was almost overwhelming.
But she forced herself to stay still, her breathing shallow under the tight corset and mask.

Later that evening, Mara sat beside Philippe, her posture as rigid as the women's chair she was locked into.
The chair had a narrow seat,
and its backrest had arms that slotted into the iGuardian between her shoulder blades and at the small of her back,
accommodated by narrow slits in her dress.
Once seated, she was locked in place,
held firmly until someone released her by pressing a button at the top of the chair, out of her reach.
The heavy folds of her Victorian-style dress cascaded around her, hiding the mechanics of the iGuardian and the chair.

Across the table, diplomats engaged in lively conversation,
discussing matters of state and trade as servants moved efficiently around them, serving dinner.
The rich aromas of the meal filled the air, but Mara couldn’t partake in the same way.
Her nourishment came through the feed port in her mask.

A maid approached Mara silently and attached an elegantly designed bag to the feed port.
The bag was filled with a nutrient liquid—a new technology
designed to ensure wards received their sustenance in a controlled and sanitary manner.
From the outside, the arrangement appeared modest, even refined, but Mara knew better.

As the maid activated the flow, the feed gag inside Mara’s mouth began to slowly fill and expand,
extending deep into her throat.
Eventually, it would fill completely, spurting the liquid down her throat before shrinking slightly.
The sensation was foreign and deeply uncomfortable, with the gag pressing against the back of her mouth.
The liquid filled her throat in spurts that required her full concentration to manage.
Mara had to time her swallows perfectly,
suppressing the natural gag reflex that threatened to overwhelm her with each pulse.

Her hands, folded neatly in her lap, trembled slightly as she focused on the mechanical process of swallowing.
The humiliation burned inside her, even though the entire procedure was hidden beneath the mask and bonnet.
She could see Philippe out of the corner of her eye, engaged in conversation with the diplomat beside him.
His voice carried calmly across the table, effortlessly authoritative.

“This feed bag arrangement is quite advantageous,” Philippe explained to the diplomat, his tone almost conversational.
“It allows for precise control over Mara’s intake,
ensuring she receives the exact nutrients she needs without the mess or unpredictability of conventional eating.
Everything is logged—calories, vitamins, minerals.
It’s much healthier for her, and it allows me to provide the care that’s expected of a guardian.”

The diplomat, intrigued, nodded thoughtfully.
“Remarkable.
I’ve heard of such systems, but I hadn’t seen one in use until now.
The technology must be a real advantage for ensuring proper care.”

“Indeed,” Philippe agreed.
“It’s a seamless way to manage Mara’s health.
She doesn’t need to worry about overindulgence or lack of nutrients, and I can monitor her well-being closely.
It’s all part of the responsibility I have to ensure she’s kept in the best possible condition.
And it's convenient for her too.
It keeps her modest at all times,
removing the need for her to unmask in public or deal with the inconvenience of a privacy cabin.”

Mara’s focus remained on the rhythmic pulsing of the gag.
Each time it released a portion of the nutrient liquid, she braced herself,
swallowing quickly to avoid the discomfort of the liquid pooling in her mouth and blocking her breathing.
Her throat worked mechanically,
the taste of the nutrient bland and slightly salty—nothing like the rich flavors of the dinner
being served to the others.

Despite the humiliation, Mara knew she had to maintain her composure.
Her role was to sit quietly, hands folded, posture perfect,
and to endure the feeding process with the same grace and submission expected of her in every other aspect of her life.
The discomfort, the control, the loss of even this basic autonomy—it all served as a constant reminder of her place.

Philippe turned slightly toward Mara, resting his hand affectionately on the back of her chair.
“Mara is adapting well to this new system,” he continued,
addressing the diplomat but clearly speaking as much for Mara’s benefit.
“It’s a matter of training, really.
Teaching her body to accept and process the nourishment in this way.
She’s made excellent progress.”

The diplomat smiled approvingly. “You’re doing an admirable job, Philippe. It’s clear she’s in very good hands.”

Mara’s eyes remained fixed forward, her breathing steady and controlled.
She swallowed again, the nutrient liquid sliding down her throat,
and she forced herself to focus on the rhythm, on the routine of it.

Much later that evening, after the dinner was over and the guests had said their goodbyes, Mara finally lay in bed,
her body encased in the rigid shell of the iGuardian.
It was her first night experiencing the full extent of the device’s restrictions,
and every sensation was new and unsettling.
Philippe had undressed her down to mask, gloves, stockings, and boots,
with the corset, neck corset, and shoulder braces relaxed for the night.

He attached restraints to her legs, locking them apart by about a meter,
and connected a restraint and charging cable to the attachment point between her shoulder blades.
“There,” he said quietly.
“You should be able to rest comfortably now, but you’ll always be aware of your boundaries.”
Mara could only lie still as Philippe finished his adjustments.

As Philippe stepped back, satisfied with his work, he gave her a brief nod. “Good night, Mara. Rest well.”

“Good night, Sir,” Mara signed, her hands moving slowly.

Left alone in the darkness, Mara explored the iGuardian with her gloved hands,
trying to familiarize herself with the device’s contours.
Her fingers traced the hard lines of the breast covers, the smooth, unyielding surface of the crotch cover.
But as soon as her touch lingered too close to these areas,
a searing pain shot through her from the electrodes embedded in the device.
It was a sharp, intense correction, reminding her not to breach her chastity device's strict limits.

Gasping silently, Mara quickly withdrew her hands, the lesson clear.
Any attempt to touch herself, even unintentionally, would be met with severe consequences.
Chastity was not just expected; it was enforced with an iron grip.
She learned to keep her hands far from the restricted areas, every movement calculated to avoid another correction.

The hours of the night stretched on, each moment a lesson in stillness and restraint.
The tension in her body never fully eased,
the ever-present pressure of the iGuardian and the restraints keeping her on edge, even in sleep.

When morning came, the device automatically released Mara from her night’s confinement.
The restraint at her back unclipped with a soft click, and the ankle restraints retracted,
allowing her to move again, though the iGuardian remained securely in place.

Mara carefully sat up,
a task made challenging by the rigid iGuardian that prevented her from bending or twisting her torso.
She had to shift her legs to the side and use her arms to push herself up.
As she moved to the edge of the bed, she used both hands to ease the pressure from the crotch cover.
Though her corset had been slightly loosened for sleep,
it still required her full attention as she maneuvered out of bed.

As soon as she stood, the corset, braces, and neck support automatically tightened to their daytime settings.
The sudden adjustment nearly threw her off balance.
Dressing herself was even harder now in the iGuardian than it had been before,
but Mara tried to handle it alone, clinging to the small bit of independence it allowed her.

She quickly dressed,
putting on her Yoyah Sheengzoh and a Victorian-style dressing gown over her gloves, stockings, and boots.
With this daily routine finished, she headed to the kitchen to prepare Philippe’s morning tea.
Every move was cautious, knowing that any mistake could trigger a painful correction from the iGuardian.
Balancing the tray, she walked carefully down the hallway,
keeping her posture as perfect as the device demanded, before reaching Philippe’s study.

Entering the study, she found Philippe already seated at his desk, reading through a stack of documents.
She paused, steadying herself before she moved forward.
Both hands full with the tray of tea, she couldn’t sign, so she approached silently.

Philippe glanced up, a slight smile on his lips. “Good morning, Mara. You may serve the tea.”

Mara positioned herself beside the table, moving with care.
She knew from the day before how crucial it was to maintain the correct posture at all times.
The iGuardian was unforgiving in its expectations.

She bent at the knees, keeping herself upright, and managed to set the cup down without triggering a correction.
The relief was brief, but she felt a small measure of satisfaction in completing the task without incident.

Philippe observed her closely, his gaze sharp as he took in every detail.
“You’re improving,” he noted with approval.
“Who would have thought that all you needed was structure and a bit of discipline?”

“Thank you, Sir, and a good morning to you, too,” Mara signed, her hands finally free.
Inside, she felt the familiar mix of frustration and resignation.
Every movement took so much effort, so much attention, that even the simplest tasks left her exhausted.
Yet, she knew she had no choice but to continue, to adapt to these new restrictions until they became second nature.

As she stood back, waiting for Philippe’s next instruction,
she reminded herself of the lessons learned so far.
The iGuardian was not just a device—it was her life now, dictating every aspect of her existence.
To survive, she had to find peace in the stillness and in the careful, deliberate movements it demanded.
And so, Mara began to learn, each day a lesson in the restraint, submission, and formality that her role required.
