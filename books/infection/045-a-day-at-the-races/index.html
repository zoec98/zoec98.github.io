                                                                                                                                                  A Day at the Races#

                                                                                                                                                  The opportunity came the following weekend when Philippe and Henry took their wards to the pony track. As they arrived, the place buzzed with excitement and order. The clinking of metal and leather being adjusted added to the strict atmosphere.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna was already on the track, dressed in her full pony outfit. Her catsuit was a striking shade of deep green, highlighting her toned body. The suit was seamless, with built-in gloves and feet, and her hair was pulled through a hole in her open-faced hood, cascading down her back. The suit left her derrière exposed, with only her chastity belt offering any modesty—a tempting target for the crop.

                                                                                                                                                  Over the catsuit, she wore a thick leather corset, dyed dark brown, cinching her waist tightly. It had attachment points at her hips for the sulky.

                                                                                                                                                  Her head was encased in an elaborate bridle, with cheekpieces, a noseband, and a throatlatch holding a bit in her mouth. The bit acted as a gag, preventing any speech. Originating from a discipline and obedience training program, it was made from rubber, but contained steel needles on the inside. It guided her, but if she resisted, the needles pressed into her mouth.

                                                                                                                                                  A wide, thick leather collar around her neck prevented any head movement, forcing Anna to follow the reins with her whole body. Blinkers attached to the cheekpieces restricted her vision, making her rely entirely on Julien’s guidance. An impressive plume matching her catsuit crowned her headstall.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna’s hands were locked to the sides of her corset with rigid leather arm sheaths, rendering them useless. Her feet were encased in knee-high pony boots, forcing her into an elevated foot position. The boots had heavy irons underneath.

                                                                                                                                                  Julien, her guardian and driver, adjusted the final straps and reins with care. He tested their tension with a gentle tug, ensuring they were responsive without immediately causing pain.

                                                                                                                                                  “Ready, Anna?” Julien asked, knowing she couldn’t respond verbally.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna’s gaze, narrowed by the blinkers, remained fixed ahead as she stood perfectly still, waiting for his command. Julien attached the sulky to her corset, securing it firmly and testing the connection. Satisfied, he took his place in the sulky, reins in hand.

                                                                                                                                                  “Walk on,” Julien commanded, touching Anna’s rear with the crop and letting the rein drop a bit.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna responded instantly, her body moving forward at the signal. She pulled the sulky towards the oval racetrack, her steps slow and cautious due to the weight of the cart and her restrictive outfit. Julien used the crop again, gently tapping her exposed rear, urging her to pick up the pace.

                                                                                                                                                  “Good girl, Anna. Keep going!” Julien encouraged.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna increased her pace, her legs working harder despite the strain of the pony boots and the weight of the irons. The blinkers limited her view, but she moved confidently, trusting Julien’s guidance. As they reached the track, Julien used the crop more frequently, tapping her rear to signal a faster pace.

                                                                                                                                                  “Go, go, go! Trot!” he ordered, his voice calm but firm.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna shifted to a trot. The irons under her boots clinked with each step, and her muscles strained under the effort. Julien maintained a gentle but firm grip on the reins, guiding her with precision. Any hint of disobedience was met with a sharper pull on the reins, a painful reminder to stay focused and compliant. Occasionally, he used the crop more sternly, delivering stinging corrections to her exposed rear.

                                                                                                                                                  Philippe and Henry watched with keen interest, noting Julien’s balanced approach of care and discipline with Anna. Philippe turned to Henry, clearly approving. “She’s well-trained. Julien has done an excellent job with her,” Philippe remarked.

                                                                                                                                                  Henry nodded, his eyes on Anna. “Indeed. She’s adapted well to the training. The discipline and control are impressive.”

                                                                                                                                                  Mara and Sonia, standing beside their guardians, observed silently. Mara was especially intrigued by the intensity of the training and the level of control Julien maintained. Sonia signed to Mara, her eyes wide with fascination.

                                                                                                                                                  “I’d like to try that someday. It looks intense but rewarding.”

                                                                                                                                                  Mara nodded, clearly interested. “Maybe we can arrange a session. It would be a good challenge.”

                                                                                                                                                  As Julien guided Anna around the track, her pace steadily increased. The physical and mental demands of the training were clear in her every movement. Despite the harshness of the exercise, there was a sense of purpose in her stride.

                                                                                                                                                  “Let’s pick up the pace. Go, go, go,” Julien commanded, giving Anna free rein.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna responded, her steps becoming more rhythmic and powerful. Julien’s guidance remained steady, his commands clear. The sulky moved smoothly behind her, showing off her strength and training.

                                                                                                                                                  After completing a fast-paced lap, Julien pulled the reins in a bit, slowing Anna to a controlled trot. The session continued, with Julien alternating between encouraging touches and sharper corrections, keeping a balance between discipline and support. After half an hour, it was clear Anna had reached her limits.

                                                                                                                                                  Philippe and Henry exchanged glances, impressed by the display of control and endurance. They knew this kind of training required not just physical strength but also mental toughness—qualities they valued highly in their wards.

                                                                                                                                                  As the session ended, Julien slowed Anna to a walk, allowing her to cool down. He dismounted and began unbuckling the straps, removing the reins. Anna stood still, her body trembling with exhaustion but her posture disciplined.

                                                                                                                                                  “Good work, Anna,” Julien said gently. “You did well today. Good girl!” Anna’s eyes, visible through the blinkers, showed her relief. She closed them as the praise triggered her conditioned response. The training had been tough, but she had endured and performed admirably.

                                                                                                                                                  Philippe turned to Mara, his voice filled with anticipation. “Are you interested in trying this, Mara? I have the necessary attachments for your iGuardian. It will be challenging, but I believe you can do it.”

                                                                                                                                                  Mara nodded, her eyes reflecting her determination. “Yes, Sir. I’m ready,” her hands danced.

                                                                                                                                                  Sonia looked startled, her hands signaling confusion. “Why would you need… Oh, you’re in a ‘Pro Secure’? I didn’t… That’s a lot more discipline than I expected.”

                                                                                                                                                  Philippe turned to her. “Yes, she is. But I have the pony training attachments available for it. We’ll see.”

                                                                                                                                                  Henry then turned to Sonia. “And you, would you like to try if we can find a fitting bridle in the stable?”

                                                                                                                                                  Sonia’s hands danced, expressing her interest. “I’d love to, Sir, but I’d rather not use someone else’s suit.”

                                                                                                                                                  Henry interrupted, “I know. The restraints were simple but effective, leaving Mara with no way to resist or adjust herself.

Her competitive spirit flared, and she was determined to win this race.

He looked over at the two sulkies, his eyes scanning both women and their drivers. The tension in the air was thick as everyone prepared for the race.
