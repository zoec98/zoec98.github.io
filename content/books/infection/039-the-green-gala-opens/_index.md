# The Green Gala opens

The day of the Green Gala was busy as Mara worked with Fleur and her staff to ensure everything was perfect.
Once the final touches were done, it was time for Mara to get ready.

Philippe had arranged a hotel suite for Mara to change, and Liliane,
a skilled dresser and makeup artist, was there to help.
Mara smiled behind her mask, noticing the floral-themed names of the staff.
Increasingly, shops and venues were putting their women into uniforms,
and the hotel was apparently taking this one step further, making the name plates part of the uniform scheme as well.

Liliane started with Mara’s hair and makeup,
enhancing her natural beauty while keeping it simple under the circumstances.
Philippe made sure her outfit was ready.
Mara undressed down to her iGuardian and then slipped into the base layer:
ballet boots and a latex suit that hugged her form.

The main part of the outfit came next.
Liliane and Philippe helped her into a stiff hobble skirt designed to look like the bark of an ancient tree.
The skirt was so tight it kept Mara’s legs close together, forcing her to take tiny steps.
The dress continued upward, covering her torso and face in bark-like material.
Her face was entirely hidden, except for a small, concealed feed port a and tiny eye holes.
The headdress was a large, green crown that resembled the leafy top of a tree.

Her arms were also covered in the bark-like material, which gave them the look of moss-covered tree branches.
Though her movements were slow and restricted, she could still use her arms to sign,
even if gripping things was impossible.

When Mara looked in the mirror, she saw a figure that resembled an ancient moving tree.
The stiffness of the dress and the bark-like covering made it clear she wouldn't be able to sit or move quickly.
She felt a strange mix of vulnerability and security,
knowing she was silent and slow, yet still able to communicate with her hands.

Philippe noticed her taking in her reflection. "Are you comfortable, Mara?" he asked gently.

"Yes, Sir. It’s different, but I’ll manage," she signed.

Philippe smiled and placed a reassuring hand on her shoulder.
"You look stunning.
I know it’s a demanding dress.
Keep in mind that I’m here to help you through the evening."

Mara took a small step, feeling the stiffness of the dress and the restriction in her movement.
"Thank you, Sir.
I appreciate your support."

As they prepared to leave the suite, Philippe guided her with a firm yet caring touch,
making sure she felt secure despite her limited movement.
The tight hobble skirt, heavy headdress, and stiff covering made every movement slow and steady.
But within the restrictions, there was a comfort in knowing Philippe was there to guide her.

"Let's make this an evening to remember,"
Philippe said as he led Mara to the lift that would take them down to the entrance.

As guests began to arrive, Mara and Philippe stood at the entrance of the grand ballroom, ready to greet them.
The guests showcased the unique blend of elegance and restriction that defined Changed society.

Some guests arrived in ballgowns with floral Victorian skirts or hair adorned with green vines,
embodying the theme of Nature's Elegance.
They curtsied politely to Mara and Philippe, exchanging formal pleasantries.

*Lord Hargreaves' Caroline emerged from the limousine,
the back of her dress rising and engulfing her in a protective shell.*

Then came Lord Hargreaves, the well-known British philanthropist.
He helped his ward, Caroline, out of the limousine.
She emerged in a complicated white dress reminiscent of lotus petals,
scandalously leaving her shoulders, head, face, and hair uncovered.

But Mara barely caught a glimpse of Caroline’s slender body and platinum blonde hair
because the back of her dress came to life.
It rose above her head, grabbed her, and engulfed her,
forming a pearlescent protective shell around her, leaving only a small slit for her to see through.
Lord Hargreaves guided her with a gentle yet firm hand.

![](white-flower-closed.jpg)

*Caroline's dress clammed shut, engulfing her in a protective shell, leaving only a small slit for her to see through.*

"Good evening, Lord Hargreaves," Philippe greeted respectfully. "Caroline, your outfit is exquisite."

"Thank you, Philippe," Lord Hargreaves replied proudly.
"Bioengineering is making fantastic progress these days.
It helps us stay alive and creates art and fashion."

Mara was impressed by Caroline's living, breathing piece of art. “You look beautiful, Caroline.”

Caroline's enclosed form curtsied, and Mara heard a faint murmur from the shell, "Thank you, Madam Mara."

The next limousine arrived, and Henry and Sonia emerged.
Philippe smiled warmly at their elegant ensembles.
Sonia's dress was a stunning blend of latex and silk in varying shades of green, adorned with real bamboo pieces.
The bodice was a high-necked corset with intricate patterns mimicking bamboo stalks,
tightly laced to enforce an impeccable posture.
The skirt, layered with latex leaves, created the illusion of a flowing bamboo forest,
but it narrowed towards the hem, forming a restrictive hobble skirt.

Sonia's sleeves were long and fitted, ending in delicate mittens connected to the dress with thin, decorative chains,
ensuring her hands remained at her sides.
A high, stiff collar framed her neck, leading to a beautifully crafted mask shaped like bamboo leaves,
decorated with small, sparkling emeralds.
The mask covered her face but left her mouth free.
Her hair was styled into an elegant updo, adorned with bamboo-shaped hairpins.

Henry wore a classical suit in shades of green and gold to complement Sonia's dress.
His suit was tailored to perfection, exuding elegance and authority.
Henry helped Sonia out of the car and guided her with a gentle but firm hand on her back.

"Good evening, Henry," Philippe greeted warmly. "Sonia, you look absolutely enchanting."

Sonia, feeling both restricted and supported, curtsied with Henry's assistance.
"Good evening, Sir.
Thank you for your kind words.
The theme of nature's elegance truly inspired us."

Mara admired Sonia’s dress. “Your outfit is a masterpiece, Sonia. The bamboo elements are striking.”

"Thank you, Mara," Sonia replied gently. "We wanted to embrace the theme as fully as possible."

Henry nodded in agreement, his eyes twinkling with pride.
"Maylee Shufoo outdid themselves with the 'Enchanted Bamboo'.
It’s both beautiful and symbolic of our commitment to sustainability."

Philippe nodded appreciatively.
"Indeed.
The craftsmanship is remarkable.
I'm pleased to see such dedication to the theme."

As Henry guided Sonia further into the ballroom, William Lemieux and his ward, Helena, arrived.
Helena was encased in a thick black latex catsuit that covered her from head to toe, with matching eye covers.
The only visible openings were the breathing holes in her nose and a cleverly hidden feed port under her left ear.
Over the latex, she wore a wide skirt made from layers of transparent white gauze and a flowing harem blouse,
revealing the shiny latex beneath.

"Sir William," Philippe greeted warmly. "Your ward's ensemble is truly remarkable."

"Thank you, Philippe," William replied proudly. "Helena put a lot of effort into this."

Mara looked at Helena, her voice gentle. "Helena, you look stunning. The combination of textures is beautiful."

Helena nodded, the faint outline of her smile visible through the latex.
She quickly looked to William, and when he nodded assent, she signed.
"Thank you, Mara.
You look enchanting as well."

The excitement grew as celebrity guests arrived.
Photographers and reporters buzzed with anticipation, their cameras ready.
Harry Styles was the first to emerge from an electric limo.

Harry's outfit caught everyone's attention.
He wore a formal Victorian tailcoat made entirely of living green leaves.
Reporters flocked to him, curious and admiring.

"Harry, can you tell us about your outfit?" one reporter asked.

Harry smiled, turning slightly for the cameras.
"This dress is a creation of BioGreen Innovations," he explained.
"It was grown on me using the latest British biotech.
The leaves are alive and will keep growing throughout the evening.
It's all about celebrating nature and sustainability."

The name BioGreen Innovations quickly became a buzzword among the reporters,
who marveled at the living, breathing masterpiece.
The dress showcased cutting-edge technology perfectly aligned with the Green Gala theme.

The crowd's attention shifted as a loud whirring announced Zendaya's arrival in an electric hexacopter drone.
She stepped out, drawing gasps from the crowd.

![](zendaya-suit.jpg)

*Zendaya stepped in front of the crowd with her head and face uncovered.*

Zendaya wore a metallic red full-body suit that highlighted her curves.
Flowers and leaves sprouted from the suit's joints, symbolizing nature reclaiming its place.
A crown of thorns and flowers sat on her head, barely covering her hair.
Her outfit represented the blend of technology and nature.

Reporters quickly noticed the most shocking detail.
"Zendaya, you’re not wearing a mask or head covering.
This is unprecedented!"

Her bare face and uncovered hair stood out against the strict norms of Changed society. She smiled confidently.
"Tonight is about celebrating harmony between nature and technology.
My outfit represents the decay of old technologies and the resurgence of nature. It's about balance."

Mara watched from beside Philippe, feeling worried about Zendaya's bold move.
"Her outfit is fantastic, but Zendaya's face-immodesty has me deeply worried.
Quick, what can we do?"
Bound and restricted, Mara couldn't intervene.

Disapproval spread through the crowd.
Reporters and traditional guests were taken aback by Zendaya's appearance.
Whispers of scandal filled the air, and photographers captured the growing discontent.

Event staff acting as an informal "fashion police," approached Zendaya, veils and masks in hand.
Before they reached her, Tom Holland emerged from the hexacopter, looking concerned.
"Zendaya, wait!" he called, hurrying after her with the missing piece of her outfit—a robot head.

The staff paused, allowing Tom to catch up.
"I told you to wait and dress properly.
This is unacceptable," he said quietly but sternly.

He retrieved the headpiece and, with the help of the staff, carefully locked it into place.
The headpiece completed her outfit, rendering her modestly covered, gagged, and unable to speak.

"Zendaya, you know the expectations," Tom scolded.
"You will always be dressed properly, especially at such a high-profile event."

Mara observed as Zendaya hand-danced her apologies, her fingers moving with contrition.
"I apologize, Sir," Zendaya's hands conveyed.
"I thought the theme would allow for more freedom.
I didn't mean to cause trouble."

Tom's eyes softened slightly, but he remained firm.
"You will always be appropriately covered.
Now, let’s proceed with the evening as planned."

With her head now covered, Zendaya was silenced and her presence became less of a focal point.
Tom's decisive action calmed the audience, who began to relax and refocus on the evening's festivities.

Throughout the evening, Tom stayed close to Zendaya, ensuring she adhered to societal expectations.
He guided her through the ballroom, managing their interactions with calm authority.
Traditional guests nodded approvingly, seeing his actions as a reaffirmation of their values.

Mara watched, sensing an opportunity to control the media narrative.
She turned to Philippe.
"Sir, perhaps we could frame this episode as a bold statement aligned with the theme.
It would help manage public perception and highlight the event's focus on harmony between nature and technology.
Zendaya's outfit could be increasingly restrictive, …"

Philippe nodded thoughtfully. "An excellent idea, Mara. Here’s how we will handle it."

He faced her fully, his eyes serious.
"We will present Zendaya’s initial appearance as a deliberate artistic choice,
symbolizing the balance between freedom and restraint, technology and nature.
I’ll speak to the press and explain that her outfit was designed to evolve throughout the evening,
representing the dynamic relationship between our natural world and the technologies we create."

Philippe continued, calm and authoritative.
"I will highlight the thematic relevance,
emphasizing that the transformation of her outfit was part of the evening's narrative.
We need to restrain ourselves and always keep discipline to live in harmony with nature.
This will frame the situation positively, showcasing our event's innovative spirit and commitment to sustainability."

Mara’s eyes sparkled with admiration.
“Thank you, Sir.
Your approach will help control the narrative.
Maybe you want to inform Tom Holland of that approach,
and suggest he plays along, restraining Zendaya more as the evening advances?”

Philippe smiled, placing a gentle hand on her shoulder.
"Excellent suggestion.
You have a keen eye for strategy, Mara.
Together, we will ensure this evening is remembered for all the right reasons."
