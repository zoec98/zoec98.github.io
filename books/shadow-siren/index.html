                      Shadow Siren

                      Storycodes

                      Mf, noncon, bdsm, kidnapping, torture, humiliation, bondage, latex, gagged, breathplay, electro, forced oral, confinement, caged, public use, objectification, mindbreak, sci-fi nanotech, tentacles, orgasm control, heist, slavery, degradation

                      A master thief stole a crown and lost her freedom, becoming a rich collector’s living trophy.

                      Keiko Toda, the Shadow Siren, slipped into Viktor Hartmann’s villa to take the Diadem of Lyria and expose the monster behind the mansion. The heist succeeded, then a living black nanomaterial trapped her and delivered her to Hartmann’s hands. Stripped of control and paraded through weeks of cages, orders, and party tricks, Keiko endured calculated punishments while the diadem mocked her on her own head. This is a dark, sci-fi captivity tale about power, sadism, public use, and the slow breaking of a thief who once fought for justice.
