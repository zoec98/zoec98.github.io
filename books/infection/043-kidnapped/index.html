                                                                                                                                                  Chapter 43: Kidnapped!

                                                                                                                                                  • Kidnapped!

                                                                                                                                                  Kidnapped!#

                                                                                                                                                  The Green Gala at the Ecoville Hotel was ending. Guests, still excited, made their way to their vehicles. Zendaya, in her metallic red fembot suit covered with flowers and leaves, approached her hexacopter with Tom Holland’s help. The suit was now almost completely locked, making her unable to move without assistance.

                                                                                                                                                  As Tom helped Zendaya into the hexacopter, the sudden noise of engines pierced the night air. Two black motorbikes stopped nearby. The riders, four figures in dark clothing and wearing advanced breather masks, leaped off their bikes.

                                                                                                                                                  “Get away from her!” Tom shouted, stepping forward. One assailant shoved him to the ground.

                                                                                                                                                  “Get back, everybody!” a masked figure barked, waving a compact, but dangerously looking weapon. The crowd scattered in fear. The hexacopter’s engines buzzed to life, the blades spinning faster and faster. Zendaya, struggling in her suit, was secured in a seat, her eyes wide with shock behind the fembot hood.

                                                                                                                                                  “Call the Grenadiers!” someone shouted. Philippe, watching from a distance, pulled out his cellphone and called for help. “We need immediate assistance at the Ecoville Hotel! There’s been a kidnapping!”

                                                                                                                                                  As the hexacopter lifted off, the two remaining attackers revved their engines and sped away on their motorbikes, weaving through the guests, and speeding down the grand driveway. Sirens echoed as the Grenadiers arrived with flashing light.

                                                                                                                                                  “Don’t let them escape!” an officer yelled. The chase began through Geneva’s narrow streets. the bikes navigated the network of sidestreets, making sharp turns. The Grenadiers followed closely, using advanced tracking systems.

                                                                                                                                                  Eliza, one of the bikers, looked back. “They’re gaining on us, Max! We need to split up!” she shouted.

                                                                                                                                                  Max nodded. “Take the left alley! I’ll draw them away!” He veered right, leading some Grenadiers away. Eliza sped down a narrow alley, her bike skidding.

                                                                                                                                                  The Grenadiers split up, some following Max, others tailing Eliza. Max turned a final corner, saw Grenadiers in front of him, while others caught up behind him. Realizing he was cornered, he skidded to a stop, his bike crashing against a wall. He stood weapon in hand as the Grenadiers surrounded him.

                                                                                                                                                  “Drop the weapon! You’re surrounded!” a Grenadier shouted.

                                                                                                                                                  Max hesitated, but realized he was outnumbered and outgunned. He dropped the weapon, raising his hands in surrender.

                                                                                                                                                  Eliza, trapped in a cul-de-sac, tried to turn back but was blocked. She revved her engine, considering a reckless escape, but the Grenadiers had her surrounded. “Stand down! There’s no way out,” an officer said as he stepped forward. Eliza recognized him—Captain Duval, a man she had once respected before the Change. She sighed, knowing the fight was over, dismounted and raised her hands.

                                                                                                                                                  Inside the hexacopter, Zendaya struggled, held down by a female captor. The male captor, piloting the craft, flew unsteadily. Zendaya’s muffled attempts to speak went unheard. The female captor tightened her grip. “Stay still, and it will be easier for you,” she whispered.

                                                                                                                                                  The hexacopter flew low over the dark waters of the lake, then following the River Rhône. The copter almost skimming treetops, weaving through narrow valleys. The absence of any pursuers confused them, but they didn’t have time to question their luck, when they eventually veered into a side-valley, up the Lonza River as they approached the Aletsch Glacier. Suddenly, the pilot executed a sharp dive, bringing the copter down towards a tiny, newly built hut next to a small landing pad. The lading was rough in the winds of the high alps,and the pilot exhaled in relief and ordered, “Get her out, quickly.”

                                                                                                                                                  The female captor dragged Zendaya across the landing pad into the hut, securing her to a chair. “We’ll be safe here for now,” she said, her voice muffled by her breather.

                                                                                                                                                  Outside, the male captor secured and folded the hexacopter, concealing it from prying eyes. But this high up they were alone, the sound of distant running water was the only he could hear.

                                                                                                                                                  Inside, Zendaya’s eyes searched for a way out. The gagged fembot mask restricted her vision, and she was throughly bound. Her female captor watched the door nervously.

                                                                                                                                                  “We have to move quickly,” the male captor said as he entered the hut, closing the door behind him. “The Freeborn Alliance will send someone to pick us up soon.”

                                                                                                                                                  The man moved towards Zendaya, removing her robot headpiece with special tooling. Zendaya gasped for breath, her voice finally freed. “Help me, please,” she cried, tears streaming down her face. “It hurts.”

                                                                                                                                                  The man frowned confused. “What hurts?”

                                                                                                                                                  “My belt,” she whimpered. “I am outside the geofence. It keeps zapping me.”

                                                                                                                                                  A stream of expletives flew from the man’s mouth. “Damn it! No wonder we weren’t followed,” he muttered. “She’s in an active chastity device. They all have tracking beacons!”

                                                                                                                                                  He looked at his female companion. “Quick! Help me get this off her! We need to silence the beacon!”

                                                                                                                                                  They fumbled with her suit as Zendaya’s sobs filled the hut while they worked, the pain from the belt’s zaps making her body twitch involuntarily.

                                                                                                                                                  Suddenly, flashbang grenades exploded, blinding and deafening them. The kidnappers were disoriented, stumbling back and covering their ears. The door burst open, and a team of Commandement des Forces Spéciales, equipped with goggles and tactical gear, stormed in, firing stun ammo at the two kidnappers. The air was filled with shouted commands and the thud of boots as doors were kicked in.

                                                                                                                                                  “Clear!” one of the Grenadiers shouted, securing the room.

                                                                                                                                                  Outside, the sound of helicopter blades grew louder. An alpine rescue emergency doctor abseiled down, followed by more heavily armed special forces. The kidnappers were bound, dragged out of the hut, and lifted into the helicopter.

                                                                                                                                                  The emergency doctor quickly assessed Zendaya, and cut through her suit and belt using specialized equipment, finally freeing her. Zendaya winced as the belt came off, still crying from the pain. “You’re going to be okay,” the doctor said softly, applying a mild sedative and wrapping her in a warm blanket. She was lifted out of the hut on a rescue stretcher and pulled up into the helicopter.

                                                                                                                                                  As the helicopter ascended and headed towards Geneva, Zendaya’s eyes fluttered closed, exhaustion and the sedative pulling her under. The ordeal was over, and she was safe again.
