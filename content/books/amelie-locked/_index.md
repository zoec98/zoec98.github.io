Amélie Valois, born in Silly (Silly-sur-Nied, Lorraine).
Her mother is a teacher,
her father a civil engineer and second-generation owner of a regional construction and materials company.
The house is bookish, courteous, and rule-forward.
Amélie grows up quiet and reliable; her music teacher trusts her with the keys and expensive violins,
her headmistress drafts her to “host” visiting officials.
She is generous and exacting, and she uses her authority quietly but relentlessly when needed.
She makes it clear that life runs more smoothly for everyone when those around her do as she says.

At 18 she leaves home to study in Strasbourg and Metz—business and applied mathematics,
