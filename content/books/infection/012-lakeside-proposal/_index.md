# Lakeside Proposal

Saturday at the Lakeside Café was like a dance for Anna and Julien, each step affirming their roles.
Julien, ever the gentleman, offered his arm to Anna as they approached the café,
his attentiveness making her feel cherished and protected.

Navigating the café in her "dress to impress" outfit required Julien's assistance.
Anna wore a stunning S-line corset with integrated shoulder braces and a Gowbao Yoohoo blouse.
The corset cinched her waist, emphasizing her figure,
while the blouse featured a high, reinforced collar that kept her head held high and her gaze demurely lowered.

As they found a spot to sit, Julien carefully guided Anna to her chair.
The layered underskirt and tight corset made sitting a calculated act.
Julien had to pull the chair out far enough for Anna to back into it gracefully,
aligning herself with the seat before slowly descending, perched delicately on the edge.
Once seated, the corset enforced an upright posture, with the shoulder braces preventing any slouching.
Her legs, concealed by the voluminous skirts, were gently pressed together by the underskirt,
the fabrics rustling softly as she adjusted to find a modicum of comfort.
Julien then leaned in to adjust the skirts, ensuring they draped elegantly around her,
providing a final touch to her poised presentation.

After that, she was pretty much stuck, arranged in her seat, upright and presenting herself to Julien seated opposite.
She would be looking at him, or rather at his hands.
The Gowbao Yoohoo locked her head into a position looking forward and downward,
and she was unable to turn it without turning her full body.
She was also unable to lift her head much,
and would be looking at him by turning her eyes upwards for a moment and looking through the lashes of her eyes.
Her pose lent itself naturally to placing her gloved hands folded into her lap,
keeping the knees under her skirt closely together,
both feet planted firmly next to each other under her perched position.

This moment, with Julien tending to her every need, underscored Anna's feeling of being intensely cared for.
However, it was more than that; it was a manifestation of her choice, her control.
By choosing this outfit, Anna had embraced a form of self-expression that was as restrictive as it was liberating.
Like a poet choosing the constraints of a sonnet to convey depth and emotion,
Anna used the limitations of her dress to craft her image and control how she was perceived.
The restrictions made her more dependent on Julien, but she appreciated how this closeness enhanced their connection.

Julien ordered food without consulting her, knowing her preferences and making it a point to remember them.
What might have once bothered her about losing this bit of autonomy now felt right.

After a dinner filled with soft glances and tender care, the world around them seemed to quieten to a whisper.
Feeling the warmth of the evening envelop her, Anna noticed Julien's demeanor shift to something more serious.
As he gently took her gloved hand in his, anticipation built within her,
a tender fluttering that seemed to echo in the air between them.

"Miss Anna," Julien said, his voice filled with emotion that instantly captured her attention,
"being with you has brought such happiness and peace into my life.
Your grace, dedication, and spirit have completely won me over.
I can't imagine my future without you by my side."

He paused, looking deeply into her eyes, the unspoken question hanging in the air.
Then, with a gentleness that matched the seriousness of his words,
he asked, "Would you do me the honor of becoming my ward?
Let's share this journey, build a life together filled with respect and love.
If you say yes, I'll ask your father for his blessing, to cherish and care for you all our days."

Overwhelmed by the sincerity and depth of Julien's proposal, Anna felt a profound joy spreading through her.
Her heart full, she found her voice and simply said, "Yes, Sir."

By saying yes, Anna accepted the world the way it had become.
She knew being Julien's ward meant embracing her role and following the new rules.
But she felt at peace with this.
The idea of being Julien's "Good Girl" and living up to expectations felt right.
She saw it as bringing honor to her family and continuing their legacy.
Julien had always taken good care of her, and with him, she felt safe.

The thought of facing this changed world without Julien was daunting.
His care and understanding made her feel secure.
By accepting his proposal, Anna was choosing the path laid out by society.
She believed that with Julien, she could find happiness even in a world shaped by the virus.
