# A Giving-Away Ceremony

As Anna planned her giving-away ceremony, her emotions were all over the place.
The private talk between Julien and her father had gone smoothly, following the customs of their Changed society.
When she heard her father agreed, she felt a deep sense of gratitude and responsibility.
This was more than just a formality—it was a promise to uphold the values and traditions that shaped their lives.

Anna chose "The Jade Garden" for the ceremony,
a venue that captured the peace and beauty she wanted for her union with Julien.
Nestled in the serene landscape north of Geneva,
the tea house was famous for its beautiful gardens and calming atmosphere.
It was the perfect setting for the start of their life together,
where the natural elegance mirrored their deep commitment.

For her outfit, Anna picked a blend of traditional Changed dress with modern touches of Chinese Hanfu.
This choice showed her personal style and her respect for tradition,
as well as her openness to the evolving nature of their society.
Her corset and Yoyah Sheengzoh beneath the elegant layers symbolized her commitment to the ideals of Changed womanhood,
showing her readiness to embrace her new responsibilities and joys.

During the official ceremony, her father would hand her over to Julien,
marking her transition from "Marc Lefèvre's Anna" to "Julien Moreau's Anna," to whom she would then owe her loyalty.
The tea ceremony Anna planned for Julien afterward was more than a ritual;
it was a promise of her dedication to their future together.
The celebration would be held in the beautiful surroundings of The Jade Garden.
The guest list was small, including those who had been pillars of support and love in their lives.
Anna hoped her dear friend Sonia could attend,
her presence a symbol of the enduring friendship that had supported them through the changes in their world.

The wedding, set for eight weeks away, was a beacon of hope for Anna.
She saw it as a way to unite tradition and love in a changing world.
