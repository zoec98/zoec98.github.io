# Anna and Julien in London

Upon their arrival in London, Anna and Julien made their way to their accommodation,
a charming B&B located on Great Titchfield Street.
The small establishment was run by Mike Smith and his Wendy,
a couple deeply invested in environmentalism and community health.
Mike, an architect, and Wendy, a nutritionist, worked from home,
which meant they were almost always available to attend to their guests.

The B&B boasted a rooftop vegetable garden, a testament to their commitment to sustainability,
which added a quaint, green touch to the urban environment.
The location of the B&B was ideal,
nestled in a central area that provided easy access to Regent's Park, the British Museum,
Covent Garden, the Royal Academy, the National Gallery, and even Buckingham Palace.

Anna and Julien were given the suite, two luxuriously appointed rooms in a beautifully maintained brick house.
The ground floor housed a small upmarket boutique, which immediately caught Anna's attention.
She was thrilled at the prospect of shopping there, eager to immerse herself in British fashion.
She signed animatedly to Julien about her excitement, her gestures full of anticipation and curiosity.

The B&B was uniquely adapted to the Changed sensibilities prevalent in Britain.
One of the most distinctive features was the roof railcart system.
Small electric cars ran on rails installed along the roof of the rooms, and extended outdoors onto the street.
This system allowed for a woman to be guided around the house and even outside,
while securely attached to a leash that could be fastened to her waist or other attachment points.
Looking out of their suite's window,
Anna observed that all the women outside were either accompanied by their guardians or connected to the rail system,
a sight that underscored the cultural norms of Changed Britain.

This widespread integration of this system fascinated both Anna and Julien.
It reflected a focus between protection and interdependence,
aligning well with the Changed philosophy of guardianship and care.

As they settled into their suite, Anna felt a growing excitement to explore more of what London had to offer.
She was particularly keen
to experience how the unique features of their B&B and the local fashion would enrich their honeymoon,
providing them both with insights and experiences that would deepen their understanding of Changed culture.

After settling into their suite, Julien directed Anna to unpack first.
They planned to explore the boutique downstairs,
so Julien suggested that Anna dress in attire suitable for both shopping and trying on new outfits.
His aim was to acquaint her with basic British Changed fashion,
focusing on practical yet stylish elements that would ensure her comfort and conformity to local norms.

Descending to the boutique, Anna found herself amidst an array of latex catsuits,
each designed with integrated gloves and feet, enhancing both their functionality and appeal.
The suits were sleek and form-fitting, designed to leave little to the imagination while providing complete coverage.
They experimented with several models, Anna playfully modeling the catsuits for Julien,
turning and posing as he assessed each one.
The boutique's mirror reflected her transformed silhouette,
each suit sculpting her figure into the epitome of Changed British style.

They selected three catsuits in varying thicknesses and colors: 
a thin basic black, a medium thickness mute teal,
and a signal orange in a thickness that restricted movement and exerted considerable pressure on Anna.
Each suit promised to keep Anna well protected and chic in the unpredictable British weather.

Julien was particularly intrigued by the sophisticated attachment systems available for the rail system,
which could be integrated with Anna's existing corsets.
Deciding to enhance her corsets with these modifications,
they arranged for the boutique's tailor to adapt them while they waited.
These modifications allowed a slottable metal bar to be attached at two strategic points:
between her shoulder blades or at the small of her back.
This feature enabled her corset
to connect with the rail system installed in the B&B and throughout some parts of the city.

The tailor worked efficiently, ensuring that the metal attachments were both secure and discreet,
allowing Anna to be seamlessly integrated into the local transportation system.
Julien downloaded an app onto his cellphone that connected to the rail system,
giving him the ability to track Anna's location and control her movements within the programmed areas.
The railcar system was designed to prevent any deviation, ensuring safety and adherence to predetermined paths.

Anna was intrigued and somewhat thrilled by the level of control this system afforded Julien.
The thought of being summonable at the touch of a button appealed to her.
It symbolized a new level of intimacy and connection between them, merging technology with their personal dynamics.

Equipped with her new attire,
Julien now also acquired a lockable muff for Anna that could be attached with a strong belt around her waist.
When her gloved hands slid into it, they were securely caught and locked in place.
The design featured an unlock button behind the zipper at the top of the muff.
However, this button was out of Anna's reach when she was locked into the muff,
ensuring that she would need Julien's assistance to be freed.
Alternatively, Julien could remotely unlock the muff with an app on his phone.
He could also set it up to interact with the railcart system,
so that it would unlock only after Anna had reached her destination.
She would have to lock herself into the muff in order for the rail system to allow her to leave that destination again.

To complement Anna's ensemble, Julien selected a variety of latex skirts.
Some were supported by petticoats, adding an elegant flair,
while others were designed to be inflated, becoming both voluminous and restrictive.
These skirts not only enhanced Anna's silhouette but also limited her mobility,
requiring her to take small, mincing steps.
Additionally, to cope with the frequent rain,
Julien chose several capes with hoods large enough to accommodate a hat underneath.
The capes were designed without arm slits and featured belts that, when fastened,
confined Anna's arms securely under the cape, necessitating assistance for her release.

Their shopping complete, Anna and Julien returned to the B&B where they were greeted by Wendy.
Wendy, herself securely attached to the rail system, commended Anna on the practicality of her rail system attachments.
"So many tourists let their women wander the city unguided;
it's quite a shame, really," she remarked.
Wendy's approval was not just polite;
it was an endorsement of Anna's commitment to respecting and adapting to local norms.

Wendy proved to be a fountain of knowledge about London and its customs.
She shared insights into the best places to visit, the nuances of local etiquette,
and how to navigate the city while adhering to the Changed protocols.
Her advice was invaluable, especially for Julien and Anna as they planned to explore the city in depth.

Anna appreciated Wendy's guidance and was pleased with the choices they had made at the boutique.
The new additions to her wardrobe not only prepared her for the weather and walking conditions
but also integrated her more deeply into the fabric of Changed British society.
