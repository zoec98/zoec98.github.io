# After the Gala

Mara's day ended early in the morning,
her body exhausted from the evening's events and the restrictive clothing she wore.
Every step was a struggle, and every smile carefully balanced against the demands of her dress.
The evening started with a crisis when Zendaya arrived bare-faced, breaking important social rules.
Mara managed the situation, but her relief was short-lived when Zendaya was kidnapped by unknown terrorists.
Now, as she prepared for bed, the uncertainty about Zendaya's fate left Mara deeply worried.

Philippe guided her to their suite, steadying her as she took unsteady steps.
The stiff ballet boots provided extra support, for which she felt grateful.
The recent events weighed heavily on their minds, and the anxiety was still fresh.

Entering the suite, they found Liliane asleep in a chair, still in her uniform.
Philippe gently woke her, and together they began to remove Mara's restrictive outfit.
The headdress came off first, providing a welcome relief from its weight.

Philippe's phone buzzed with a message.
His face softened with relief as he read it.
"Mara," he said softly, "Zendaya has been rescued.
All four kidnappers have been caught."

Mara's tense muscles relaxed at his words.
"Thank God," she signed.
"Do we know who they were or why they did this?"

Philippe shook his head.
"Not yet.
They were very well-equipped but badly trained.
It's unclear who they are or what they wanted."

Mara hand-danced her disappointment and unease.
"It's unsettling to think such a well-funded group is out there."

"I know," Philippe said, squeezing her hand.
"But for now, Zendaya is safe, and the immediate threat is over.
We'll find out more in time."

She tried to take comfort in his words.
"I hope so. This night was more than I expected."

Philippe offered a reassuring smile. "We'll handle it, Mara. Together."

They continued the careful process of undressing her, removing the stiff layers that encased her.
With each piece removed, Mara felt both relief and a growing sense of vulnerability.
Philippe held her steady while Liliane removed each part of her dress.

Once Mara could move freely again, Philippe and Liliane finished undressing her, leaving her in only her iGuardian.
Philippe then sent Liliane off with the pieces of Mara's dress, wishing her a good night.

The shift from the restrictive dress to the iGuardian left Mara feeling exposed.
Philippe slipped a pair of high heels onto her feet—flat shoes were out of the question after years
of wearing ballet heels.
The height felt comfortable compared to the constant strain of the ballet boots.

He then led her to the bathroom for a quick wash.
The iGuardian took care of most of the process, but Philippe hand-washed her exposed skin with gentle care.
His touch was soothing, washing away the tension of the evening.
The warmth of the water and his hands eased her aching body.
He helped her remove her mask so she could brush her teeth, and then guided her to the bed.

As was their routine, he had her kneel by the bed so he could do her hair.
He let down her bun, untangled the braids, but only did the necessary care, sensing how tired she was.
His fingers moved through her hair with skill, lulling Mara into a near-sleep state.
She was almost asleep when he relaxed the collar, braces, and corset of the iGuardian,
lifted her into bed, and cuffed her arms in front of her, ensuring she was secure but comfortable.

Mara barely noticed when Philippe spooned against her,
his body warm and comforting against the hard surface of the iGuardian.
His warmth and presence kept the nightmares of the kidnapping away.
Instead, she dreamed of the evening's successes, feeling safe knowing Philippe was there to protect her.

Mara woke the next morning, still in darkness.
The room was quiet except for Philippe's gentle breathing behind her.
She felt the firm embrace of the iGuardian, her feet elevated in heels, and the restriction of her cuffs.
But despite this, she felt vulnerable.
Her legs were bare, her arms ungloved, her mouth unfilled, and her face uncovered.
This sense of exposure unsettled her.

As she stirred, Philippe woke up, sensing her discomfort.
He shifted closer, offering comfort.
She took a deep breath and spoke softly.

"Sir, may I please have my mask?"
Her voice, almost unused over the last three years, was hoarse.
Mara hated speaking, preferring the elegance and precision of hand-dancing,
but with her hands cuffed, she had no other way to communicate.
"I feel insecure without it."

Philippe understood right away.
"Of course, Mara," he replied gently, reaching for the nightstand.
He picked up her Jurenmuse and turned back to her, placing the gag in her mouth and locking the mask to her collar.
The snug fit and pressure around her face made her feel safer.

"Thank you, Sir," Mara tried to say, but only a satisfied moan escaped.

Philippe smiled, caressing her shoulder. "You're welcome, Mara. Feel better now?"

Mara cuddled back against Philippe as much as she could within the stiff iGuardian, feeling relieved.
His warmth and the mask's security helped her relax again.

She drifted back to sleep, her mind at ease.
The protective iGuardian and Philippe's presence allowed her to surrender to the comforting darkness,
feeling loved and cared for.

The next morning, Philippe guided her through the morning cleaning and dressing routine with care.
He helped her into stockings and gloves, ensuring every seam was aligned.
After securing her high-collared blouse and hobble skirt, he put her into a Victorian bustle dress again.

“Ready, Mara?” Philippe asked softly, checking her dress one last time.

“Yes, Sir,” Mara signed.

Philippe activated the her iGuardian's guidance, and it sent her down to the breakfast room.
She stood waiting at the entrance, in line with other women waiting for their guardians.
When Philippe arrived, he led her over to his table and locked her into her chair.

A maid in the hotel's uniform, her name tag read "Iris," responded to Philippe’s signal.
She attached a feed bag to the port on Mara’s mask and began serving Philippe his breakfast.
Mara watched, feeling the slow expansion of the feed gag in her mouth, the nutrient liquid spurting down her throat.
Her swallowing was automatic, years of training establishing themselves.

A few minutes later, Sonia and Henry entered.
Henry, in a stylish suit, waved to Philippe, who gestured for them to join.

“Good morning, Philippe,” Henry greeted. “Good morning, Mara.”

“Good morning, Henry. Sonia,” Philippe replied.

Henry locked Sonia into the chair opposite Mara so that they could see each other.
Sonia’s dress was equally elaborate but close-fitting: A blouse, buttoned in the back, and a floor-length mermaid skirt.
For breakfast, she wore a niquab that would allow her to eat instead of a mask.
As soon as Sonia was secured, her eyes met Mara’s, and they began signing to each other.

“Did you hear about Zendaya?” Sonia signed, serious.

Mara nodded, signing quickly.
“Yes, Philippe told me this morning.
She’s been rescued, and all four kidnappers have been caught.”

Henry leaned forward, looking at Philippe. “What do we know about the kidnappers?”

Philippe shook his head.
“Not much yet.
They were well-equipped but poorly trained.
We don’t know who they are or their motives.
It’s unsettling, to say the least.”

Sonia signed quickly, concern in her eyes. “Poor Zendaya. Do we know how she’s doing?”

“She’s safe and apparently unharmed,” Philippe reassured her.
“She’s being taken care of and will be back soon.”

Mara and Sonia shared a deep sense of relief. “Thank goodness she’s safe. Last night was terrifying,” Mara signed.

Henry nodded. “We’ll need to stay vigilant. If there’s one group willing to go this far, there might be others.”

Philippe agreed. “Absolutely. We need to ensure everyone’s safety and be prepared for future threats.”

Sonia, sensing the need to lighten the mood, began hand-dancing with a hint of a smile.
“Did you see Zendaya’s outfit last night?
Despite everything, her transformation was fascinating.
From a scandalously bare-faced human woman to an ever more robot-like being, all while incorporating elements of nature.
It was quite a spectacle.”

Mara nodded, signing back.
“Yes, it was intriguing.
Her outfit became increasingly strict and mechanical.
It’s amazing how fashion can reflect such contrasts.
It also reminded me how important it is
to maintain flexibility and muscle strength when wearing something as restrictive as a robot suit or my iGuardian."

Sonia’s eyes lit up.
“Exactly!
The strictness of our dress demands different fitness.
It reminded me of Julien Moreau's Anna.
Before the Change, she was a runner.
Now, she’s kept in shape with pony training.
It’s rigorous but effective.”

Mara’s interest was piqued. “Pony training? I’ve heard of it but never seen it in practice. How does it work for her?”

Sonia signed enthusiastically, “It’s fascinating.
Anna wears a special suit that restricts her and has a guide bit like a pony.
She’s put in front of a sulky, and Julien guides her through an endurance course.
It’s a combination of physical training and discipline.”

Mara nodded thoughtfully.
“That sounds challenging and intriguing.
I’d love to learn more about it.
Maybe we could arrange a visit?”

Sonia’s hands moved eagerly.
“I’m sure Anna would be happy to show you.
She’s adapted well and enjoys the training now.”

Their conversation flowed seamlessly, their hands expressing what their voices couldn’t.
Henry and Philippe watched with approval, pleased to see their wards engaged.

Philippe turned to Henry.
“It’s good to see them connecting.
They have a lot to share.
Pony training sounds interesting.
Mara could use more strength exercises, she's so focused on mobility right now.”

Henry nodded warmly.
“It’s quite demanding.
It started as a discipline program for problematic women,
focusing on obedience and submission, and also on keeping them exhausted.
But a lot of well-adjusted women are doing it now, for fitness.”

Philippe and Henry agreed to explore the sport with their wards soon.
