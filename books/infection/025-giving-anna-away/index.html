                                                                                                                                                  Chapter 25: Giving Anna Away

                                                                                                                                                  • Giving Anna away

                                                                                                                                                  Giving Anna away#

                                                                                                                                                  The Jade Garden was ready, its beauty shining as it opened its doors for a Giving-Away. This place, where old elegance met modern grace, was the perfect spot for Anna and Julien to join their lives.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna walked through the garden’s lush paths, her dress rustling softly. Everyone felt the excitement, a sweet tension filled the air as the wedding moment got closer.

                                                                                                                                                  Away from the crowd, Anna and Julien had a quiet moment alone. They used hand-dancing to talk, a way they often shared their thoughts. Anna bowed to Julien, showing her deep respect and dedication. Her hand-dancing told him of her love and commitment, a message deeper than words.

                                                                                                                                                  Julien, moved by her actions, whispered “good girl” to Anna, and she felt the rush run through her body as it always did when he said that. His words confirmed her role and identity in their new life together.

                                                                                                                                                  Soon, the first guests arrived, reminding them of the love and support from friends and family. With this reassurance, Anna and Julien were ready to face their future together. The garden, with its calm pond and beautiful design, was the ideal setting for their vows. Surrounded by nature and loved ones, their commitment went beyond each other. It was a pledge to live by the values and roles they believed in.

                                                                                                                                                  As noon arrived Anna and Julien prepared to make their vows, up on a platform in front of their friends and family. Anna hand-danced her words, as rehearsed, Julien accepted with voice, using the words Anna had explained to Sonia. Her father then formally gave her away to Julien, stating in front of all that she brought honor to his family with this union, and that he now handed her over to Julien, being the first to call her officially “Julien Moreau’s Anna.”

                                                                                                                                                  After their vows, Anna was ready for the tea ceremony. This was more than a tradition for her. It was a deep expression of her new role with Julien, showing her commitment to the ideals they shared, her desire to serve and care for him. As the guests settled into silence, Anna and Julien positioned themselves on opposite sides of a low, flat table. Despite formal wedding attire, Anna moved gracefully. Her dress and every step were a part of her, showing the role she proudly accepted.

                                                                                                                                                  Anna started by cleaning the tea set, thinking of it as clearing away the past for a fresh start with Julien. This cleaning was her silent promise for a clear and honest life together. Warming the teapot and cups, she felt a vow to always be Julien’s comfort. The warmth symbolized her promise to make a loving and secure home. Choosing the tea leaves, Anna thought about their life’s choices. Making the tea was like building their relationship—complex but full of love and respect. Pouring the tea for Julien, she showed her full commitment to him. It was a sign of her respect and acceptance of her role beside him. Serving the tea, Anna completed her vow. This was her pledge to support Julien, to follow their values and his lead.

                                                                                                                                                  When Julien accepted the tea, he felt the trust Anna placed in him. The tea represented Anna’s life, given to him. He saw the importance of her commitment and felt a strong responsibility to support and protect her. Drinking the tea, Julien thought about his own vows. Anna’s dedication required him to be equally committed. He knew her acceptance of her role was a deep sign of trust, a trust he planned to honor fully.

                                                                                                                                                  It was Anna’s heart speaking, her way of showing her dedication to Julien and their shared ideals. It confirmed her belief in the harmony of their union, founded on the principles that brought them together.

                                                                                                                                                  As the ceremony ended, their guests’ applause filled the air. It celebrated the love and understanding between Anna and Julien. Anna felt a deep peace, sure in her choice to live by these ideals and perform this ritual for Julien.
